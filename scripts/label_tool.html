Quick reference
  • clean: normal messages, opinions, jokes, criticism of ideas without insults, reporting abuse without endorsing it.
  • abusive: insults or abuse aimed at a person or group.
  • profane: swearing or vulgar words not aimed at anyone.
  • religious_hate: attacks on a religion, sect or its followers.
  • sexual_hate: sexually abusive or sexist hate.
  • unsure: cannot decide after a careful read (keep under 5%). unusable: no readable Latin text.
  • If several apply, the most specific wins: religious_hate / sexual_hate, then abusive, then profane, then clean.
  • Label what the message says, not what you guess the writer meant. Full rules: docs/labeling-guide.md
